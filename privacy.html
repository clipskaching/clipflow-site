Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

ClipFlow runs on your own computer. The developer does not collect, receive or store your videos, clips, captions or account tokens.

What stays on your computer

Your source videos, generated clips, titles and descriptions, a record of what you have posted, and the access tokens that platforms give ClipFlow when you connect an account are stored in files on your computer only.

What ClipFlow sends to platforms

When you choose to publish, ClipFlow sends the selected clip file and its title, description and hashtags directly from your computer to the platform you picked, using that platform's official API. For TikTok this uses the Content Posting API with the permissions you approve (video.publish, video.upload and basic profile info). ClipFlow reads only what is needed to post (for example your account's allowed privacy options) and does not read your followers, messages or other private data.

Third parties

The platforms you connect process your data under their own privacy policies. ClipFlow does not sell or share your data with anyone else and uses no advertising or analytics trackers.

Your choices

You can disconnect any platform at any time in that platform's app-permissions settings and delete ClipFlow's local files to remove all stored tokens and data.

Children

ClipFlow is not directed at children under 13.
